# Nook Cat Care

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Nook Cat Care primarily serves cat owners in Tseung Kwan O, Sha Tin, Fo Tan and Tai Po. Hong Kong Island carries a HK$10 transport surcharge per visit. Other areas can enquire via WhatsApp to confirm service and any surcharge. The service is for cat owners, especially people who travel, work long hours, or need temporary help while their cats remain at home. The primary audience values familiar routines, cleanliness, careful handling of their home and keys, and clear evidence that each visit was completed. The service is particularly relevant to owners of shy, senior, anxious, routine-sensitive, or multiple cats.

The website is primarily Traditional Chinese with a complete English version. Visitors need to understand availability, assess trust, and book comfortably from both mobile and desktop devices.

## Product Purpose

Nook Cat Care provides home-visit cat sitting so cats can remain in the environment they know while their owners are away. Each visit covers the agreed care routine, including food, water, litter care, basic wellbeing checks, and interaction appropriate to the individual cat. Owners receive photos, videos, and visit notes for reassurance.

The website's primary job is to let customers choose a service and available dates, then send an enquiry. The team confirms each booking and arranges visit times in its reply.

## Positioning

Cat-only home care in Hong Kong that combines vetted, cat-literate sitters, transparent availability and pricing, reliable visit routines, and photo and video updates after every visit.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: send a date-only booking enquiry through the availability calendar.
- Secondary CTA: enquire through WhatsApp.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Care that feels like home."
- Belief ladder: cats experience less disruption when cared for in their familiar home; Nook understands cat behaviour and respects individual boundaries; every visit follows a clear, dependable routine; photo and video updates provide tangible reassurance; availability and pricing are transparent; booking is straightforward.
- Proof on hand: customer testimonials. Real visit photography and update examples should be added as they become available and must never be replaced by fabricated client evidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reliable, and warm. Nook should feel like a careful, cat-literate neighbour: personal and gentle without becoming childish, sentimental, or casual about responsibility. Communication is reassuring rather than dramatic, honest rather than overpromising, and specific about what owners can expect.

The emotional goal is quiet confidence. Owners should feel that their cat's routine, boundaries, home access, and wellbeing will be handled carefully while they remain informed throughout the service.

## Anti-references

Nook should not resemble a luxury pet hotel, a playful pet shop, a children's brand, or a loud sales-led marketplace. Avoid excessive cuteness, baby talk, cat puns, paw-print decoration, exaggerated claims, crowded colour, glossy corporate presentation, and overly staged pet imagery. Do not imply that every cat will be playful, happy, or stress-free, and do not use AI-generated cats as fake client examples.

## Design Principles

1. **Make trust concrete.** Show exactly what happens during a visit, how home access is handled, what updates owners receive, and what customer testimonials confirm.
2. **Follow the cat's routine.** Frame the service around familiar spaces, individual boundaries, and cat-specific care rather than generic pet sitting.
3. **Reduce booking uncertainty.** Keep availability, service coverage, pricing, and the path from date selection to confirmation clear and immediate.
4. **Reassure through evidence.** Prefer real home photography, visit reports, photos, videos, and precise service details over broad emotional claims.
5. **Be bilingual by design.** Treat Traditional Chinese as the primary experience and English as a complete parallel experience, with equal clarity and no compromised layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

There is no formal WCAG conformance target. The experience should still provide baseline keyboard usability, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, clear focus states, appropriately sized touch targets, and legible Traditional Chinese and English text across responsive layouts. Language switching must be easy to find and must preserve the visitor's place in the booking flow.
