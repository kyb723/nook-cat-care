常見問題Questions

大家成日問嘅嘢。Things people ask us.

如果我隻貓成個探訪都匿埋，點算？What if my cat hides the whole visit? +

好正常，完全冇問題。我哋唔會夾硬拉貓咪出嚟。我哋會靜靜完成日常工作，確認佢安全、有食嘢，距離由佢自己決定。大部分貓咪幾次探訪之後就會慢慢熟——按佢自己嘅時間表。 That's normal, and completely fine. We don't pull cats out from under beds. We do the practical work quietly, confirm they're safe and eating, and let them decide the distance. Most cats warm up over a few visits — on their own schedule.

鎖匙點樣處理？How do you handle my keys? +

可約見面簽收交收。鎖匙只會用編號標記——唔會寫你嘅名或者地址——旅程完結後照樣簽收交還，熟客可以選擇安全代存。 Keys are collected at the meeting with a signed handover record. They're labelled with a code — never your name or address — and returned the same way after your trip, or kept securely if you're a regular.

可唔可以幫手餵藥？Can you give medication? +

可以——口服藥、保健品同外用藥，全部跟足獸醫嘅書面指示。見面嗰日會一齊確認流程。打針我哋就唔做；有需要嘅話我哋建議搵獸醫護士，亦都可以幫你安排。 Yes — oral medication, supplements, and topical treatments, following your vet's written instructions. We'll confirm the routine at the meet & greet. We don't do injections; for those we'd recommend a vet nurse and can help you arrange one.

服務範圍係邊度？Which areas do you cover? +

港島、九龍同新界東北係標準收費，偏遠地區只需少量交通費，一定會事先報價。唔肯定你嘅地址得唔得？message 我哋，當日就有直接答覆。 Hong Kong Island and Kowloon at standard rates, and most of the Remote areas with a small travel fee quoted upfront. Not sure about your address? Message us — we'll give you a straight answer the same day.

如果有緊急情況，會點做？What happens in an emergency? +

我哋會即刻打俾你，再聯絡你嘅獸醫——兩樣都會喺見面嗰日白紙黑字寫低。如果貓咪需要送院，我哋會用佢自己嘅貓籠接送，並且留喺度直至聯絡到你為止。你唔會事後先知道有事發生。 We call you first, then your vet — both agreed at the meeting, in writing. If your cat needs transport to a clinic, we take them in their own carrier and stay until we've reached you. You'll never find out about a problem after the fact.
